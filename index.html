How to Install

From box to finished wall/ceiling in six steps

No special tools. Just a marker, a drill, and the screws in your box.

Applies to Linear Slot Diffuser
Step 1

Mark the screw holes

Hold the vent in place and mark where each screw will go.

Step 2

You'll have 4 spots

Four marks in total — one near each corner of the opening.

Step 3

Drill small pilot holes

Carefully drill a small hole at each of the four marks.

Step 4

Place the vent back

Line the vent up in the same position over the opening.

Step 5

Screw it in securely

Drive a screw into each pilot hole until the vent sits flush.

Done

Set your airflow

Slide the louvers to aim the air. That's it — you're done.
